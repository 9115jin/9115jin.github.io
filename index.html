Biography

About

I am a researcher at ETRI working on reliable and adaptive vision-language models. My research focuses on multimodal knowledge editing, visual reasoning, and evaluation of failure modes in VLMs. I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science from Hanyang University in 2022, advised by Prof. Jongwoo Lim at the Robot Vision Lab.
